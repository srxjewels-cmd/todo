Catalogue Maker

Pick the folder that holds your product photos (e.g. D:\SHREE RAM). Put photos in sub-folders — one sub-folder per category (Rings, Necklaces, …). Everything stays on your computer; nothing is uploaded. Prices are never shown.

Make a product catalogue from your photos

Organised by category, with details, and no prices.

  1. Arrange photos in sub-folders — one per category:
    D:\SHREE RAM\Rings\, D:\SHREE RAM\Necklaces\, …
  2. Click Choose photo folder… and select D:\SHREE RAM.
  3. Add details on any item if you like, then Save as PDF or Download single file.
