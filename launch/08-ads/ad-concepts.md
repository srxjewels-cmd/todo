# [BRAND] — Launch Ad Concepts, Testing Structure & Naming Conventions

**Hero SKU:** RIV-01 lab-grown diamond Rivière necklace · **Support:** lab-grown diamond studs
**Format:** all concepts shot 9:16 vertical (1080×1920), 15–25s, native/UGC-adjacent but graded to quiet-luxury (soft daylight, neutral palette, no hard TikTok text-slams — use clean serif/sans supers).
**Audience:** women 25–40, US/UK/EU. **Narrative:** fair value, honesty, permanence. **Never:** discount language, urgency countdowns, "% off", "sale".

**Global compliance rules (apply to every frame, super, caption, and voiceover):**
1. "lab-grown" immediately precedes "diamond(s)" in ALL copy — spoken and on-screen. Never "diamond" unqualified, never "lab diamond", never "diamonds (lab-grown)".
2. Every vermeil mention states base metal + thickness: "18k gold vermeil (2.5µm+ gold over sterling silver)". Short-form super allowed: "18k gold vermeil over sterling silver, 2.5µm+".
3. IGI claims only where literally true for the stones in that SKU.
4. Made-to-order / ~7-day shipping stated as "made to order · ships in ~7 days" (approximate, matches PDP).
5. No before/after skin claims, no income/investment claims about lab-grown diamonds ("holds value" is banned — ASA/FTC risk), no disparaging named competitors.

Price placeholders: **[PRICE-RIV]** (RIV-01), **[PRICE-STUDS]** (studs), **[RETAIL-COMP]** (comparable mined-diamond retail figure — must be substantiable, e.g. documented average of 3+ named-retailer quotes kept on file).

## Launch testing structure ($50–100/day total)

### Phase 0 — Prerequisites (before day 1)
- Pixel/CAPI verified per `/home/user/todo/launch/08-ads/pixel-catalog-setup.md`.
- All 4 concepts delivered as 9:16 masters + 1:1/4:5 crops; captions burned in.
- Set the **CAC guardrail** before spending: with AOV ≈ [PRICE-RIV] (hero-led) and a target of **first-order contribution margin after CAC ≥ 30%**, guardrail CAC = **[AOV × ~0.35–0.40]**. Example mechanics: if blended AOV lands at $400 and landed product+shipping+fees cost ~40%, gross margin per order ≈ $240 → cap CAC at ~$140–160, scale toward CAC ≤ $120. Founder fills real numbers; write them at the top of the ads dashboard and treat as law.

### Phase 1 — Creative testing, Meta-first (days 1–10) · ~$70–80/day Meta, ~$20–30/day TikTok
Structure (Meta): **1 campaign, ABO (ad-set budget optimisation), 4 ad sets — one per concept.**

- Why ABO not CBO/Advantage+ budget at this stage: with equalised budgets per ad set, every concept gets a fair spend allocation; CBO would starve three concepts to feed an early leader before significance.
- Each ad set: **$15–20/day**, optimisation = Purchase (do not launch on AddToCart optimisation; weak-signal buyers), Advantage+ audience with women 25–40 as suggestion, US only first (single market = cleaner read; UK/EU after winner is found and consent flow is proven).
- Each ad set contains the concept's video + its 3 primary texts / 3 headlines as separate ads or via flexible ad format — let Meta rotate copy.
- TikTok mirror (optional at $20–30/day): 1 campaign, 2 ad groups (Concept A, Concept C — the most TikTok-native), Complete Payment optimisation; expect slower learning at this budget, judge on 7-day windows.

**Learning windows & decision cadence:** evaluate every **3–4 days**, never intraday, never before ~$45–60 spent per ad set (≈3× guardrail-CAC-adjacent signal at these budgets). Judge on: CPA (7-day click / 1-day view), CTR (outbound), hook rate (3-sec views / impressions), cost per ATC as leading indicator when purchases are sparse.

**Kill rules (at each 3–4 day check, in order):**
- Spend ≥ 2× guardrail CAC with **zero purchases** → kill the ad set.
- CPA > 1.5× guardrail after ≥ 3 purchases → kill.
- Hook rate < 20% AND outbound CTR < 0.7% after ~2,000 impressions → kill the creative, keep the concept (re-cut a new hook before abandoning the angle).
- Never kill anything with < 3 days or < $45 spend unless it's a policy rejection.

**Scale rules:**
- CPA ≤ guardrail across a full 3–4 day window → raise that ad set's budget **+20–30% every 3 days** (bigger jumps reset learning).
- Two consecutive good windows → promote to Phase 2.

### Phase 2 — Consolidation (from ~day 10–14)
- Take the 1–2 winning concepts (and their best copy variants, per breakdown data) into a **single consolidated campaign with CBO / Advantage+ shopping-style setup**, budget = the majority of daily spend ($60–80/day), broad targeting, US + UK (EU once UK holds).
- Keep a **testing ad set alive forever at ~20% of budget** ($15–20/day ABO) — one new hook or concept variant per week feeds the consolidated campaign. Creative is the targeting; the test lane is not optional.
- Add a small **retargeting ad set** ($10–15/day) once pools exist (site visitors 30d, 50% video viewers, waitlist customer list): Concept D + Concept B lead here.
- Continue kill/scale rules at the campaign level; scale total spend only while blended CAC ≤ guardrail on a trailing 7 days, checking Shopify orders (source of truth) against platform-reported CPA weekly to compute the platform-inflation factor.

## Naming conventions

**Campaign:** `[BRAND]_{Platform}_{Objective}_{Funnel}_{GEO}_{YYYYMM}`
> `BRAND_META_PUR_PROSP_US_202607` · `BRAND_TT_PUR_PROSP_US_202607` · `BRAND_META_PUR_RTG_USUK_202608`

**Ad set / ad group:** `{Audience}_{Placement}_{Test-ID}`
> `BROAD-W2540_ADVPLC_T01-ConceptA` · `RTG-SITE30D_ADVPLC_T00` · `BROAD-W2540_ADVPLC_T01-ConceptD`

**Ad:** `{ConceptID}_{Format}_{Hook}_{CopyVariant}_{Version}`
> `A-MacroSparkle_916_waitforit_PT1-H2_v1` · `B-ProofMath_916_costumetap_PT3-H1_v1` · `C-QuietStack_45_mirror_PT2-H3_v2`

Rules: no spaces (underscores between fields, hyphens inside a field); IDs stable forever (a re-cut = `v2`, a new hook = new Hook token, never rename in place — it breaks reporting continuity); funnel tokens `PROSP/RTG/RTN`; objective tokens `PUR/ATC/TRF`; mirror identical names across Meta and TikTok so cross-platform reporting joins on name.
