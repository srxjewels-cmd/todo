# Creator Sourcing Playbook — [BRAND]
### Repeatable recipes to grow the seeding list from ~50 to 250 nano/micro creators (3K–50K)

Target profile: women 25–40, US/UK/EU, posting jewellery styling, "clean girl" aesthetic, quiet luxury, fashion GRWM, bridal/engagement, or lab-grown diamond education. Everything below is a recipe you can run weekly without paid tools; paid options are listed at the end.

---

## 1. Exact search queries that work

### 1a. Google → Instagram profile mining (fastest count-verified source)
Instagram profile pages expose "X Followers" in their search-result meta description, so a `site:instagram.com` search returns handle + follower count + bio in one hit. Run these verbatim in Google (or any engine), then keep only personal creators in the 3K–50K band:

- `site:instagram.com "jewelry influencer" followers minimalist`
- `site:instagram.com "jewellery blogger" UK gold`
- `site:instagram.com "UGC creator" jewellery UK`
- `site:instagram.com "wedding content creator" booking 2026`
- `site:instagram.com "personal stylist" Berlin OR Milan OR Madrid followers`
- `site:instagram.com "capsule wardrobe" coach followers`
- `site:instagram.com "clean girl" GRWM creator`
- `site:instagram.com bride to be "wedding planning" creator`
- `site:instagram.com "diamond education" creator` (filter out jewelers/brands)
- Swap city/language tokens for EU: `créatrice de contenu mode Paris`, `schmuck bloggerin Berlin`, `moda minimal Milano`, `scandi style Copenhagen`

**Filter rule:** skip accounts whose bio reads like a shop (prices, "free shipping", "DM to order", "handmade by me") — makers are competitors, not seeders. Keep people who *style, review, tour, or explain* jewellery.

### 1b. TikTok in-app search (run logged-in, Discover tab)
- `everyday jewelry tour` / `gold jewelry collection tour`
- `clean girl jewelry essentials`
- `quiet luxury jewelry`
- `lab grown diamond explained` / `lab grown vs natural diamond`
- `engagement ring shopping vlog`
- `Mejuri haul` / `Dorsey unboxing` / `Pandora lab grown review`
- `jewelry unboxing gold vermeil`
- `GRWM office outfit gold hoops`
Sort by "Most liked this month" to find rising nano creators, not legacy macros. Also run the same queries as `site:tiktok.com` Google searches — result URLs literally contain the handle (`tiktok.com/@handle/video/...`).

### 1c. Instagram in-app search
Search the phrases above in the IG search bar → Accounts tab; then for each good account open **Suggested accounts (the "+" chevron on the profile)** — IG clusters lookalike creators, and one good 8K jewellery stylist reliably surfaces 10–20 more in-band lookalikes. This chevron-crawl is the single highest-yield free method once you have 10 seeds.

## 2. Hashtag mining method (30 min/week per tag)
Tags to rotate: `#jewelrytok #labgrowndiamonds #labgrowndiamond #quietluxury #goldjewelry #jewelryaddict #everydayjewelry #earstack #ringstack #cleangirlaesthetic #GRWM #oldmoneyaesthetic #bridetobe2026 #engagementring #MusesofAM` (Astrid & Miyu's ambassador tag), `#mejuripartner`.

Method:
1. Open the tag → **Recent** (TikTok: filter by upload date), not Top. Top = macros; Recent = nano/micro.
2. Scan 50–100 posts; open every account that looks like a personal creator.
3. Qualify in 30 seconds: follower band 3K–50K → likes on recent posts ≥ 3% of followers → female 25–40 aesthetic fit → US/UK/EU location cues in bio.
4. Log qualified handles in `seeding-tracker.csv` with `status=to_review` and the hashtag as `source`.
Yield benchmark: ~10–15 qualified rows per tag-hour.

## 3. Competitor tagged-posts mining (highest intent, do this first each week)
People who already tag jewellery brands are pre-qualified: they buy, wear, and post jewellery unprompted.

1. Open the competitor profile → **Tagged** tab: @dorseyofficial (Dorsey), @mejuri, @pandora / search "Pandora lab grown" tag mentions, plus @missoma, @astridandmiyu, @monicavinader, @analuisany, @vraiofficial (VRAI lab-grown).
2. Scroll the tagged grid; skip brand reposts and mega-influencers; open personal accounts.
3. Same 30-second qualification as above. Note in the tracker which competitor they tagged (`notes` column) — outreach converts better when you reference it ("saw your Mejuri stack…").
4. Also check who comments repeatedly on competitor launch posts — superfans with 3K+ followings are ideal gifting targets.
5. TikTok equivalent: open the competitor's profile → tap any viral video → check stitches/duets, and search `"[competitor] review"` / `"[competitor] haul"`.
Yield benchmark: 15–25 qualified rows/hour, and these convert to "agreed" at the highest rate.

## 5. Lab-grown educators & jewellery writers
- YouTube search: `lab grown diamond review`, `lab grown engagement ring mistakes` → filter channels 1K–100K subs; check the About tab for email.
- Substack: search "jewelry" on substack.com — already tracked: The Foyer, Jewelry Reference, The Jewelry Casket, The Court Jeweller, Jewelry Diary, Jewelry Club Weekly, The Honesty Box. Pitch as editorial gifting (review samples + founder story), not sponsored posts.
- Podcast guests on jewellery/bridal podcasts are usually micro creators with engaged audiences.

## 6. Engagement-rate rule of thumb
**ER = (likes + comments on last 10–12 posts ÷ 10–12) ÷ followers.**
- **Accept ≥ 3%** (nano 3K–10K should hit 5–10%+).
- 2–3%: borderline — accept only if comments are substantive.
- < 2% at under 50K followers: pass (dead or bought audience).
- TikTok: use views instead — median views ≥ 20–30% of follower count is healthy.

## 7. Fake-follower red flags (check before shipping anything)
- ER under ~1–2% at micro scale, or likes wildly inconsistent post-to-post (200, 3k, 180…).
- Comments that are generic/emoji-only, or from accounts with no avatar/0 posts.
- Follower spikes overnight (check free graphs on Socialblade); steady staircase = organic.
- Following ≈ followers at scale (mass follow/unfollow farming).
- Audience mismatch: US/UK creator whose commenters are all bot-like foreign accounts.
- Bio buzzwords with no brand tags ever accepted, or engagement-pod behaviour (same 20 accounts commenting on every post).
Rule: **fewer real rows beats a padded list — never ship product to an account you haven't manually scanned.**

## 9. Weekly quota plan → 250 rows
Cadence assumes ~4 focused hours/week. Statuses: `to_review → to_contact → contacted → agreed → shipped → posted`.

| Week | New qualified rows | Cumulative | Focus |
|------|-------------------|------------|-------|
| 1 | validate the 52 starter rows + add 25 | ~75 | competitor tagged mining (Mejuri/Dorsey) |
| 2 | +50 | ~125 | hashtag mining #jewelrytok #cleangirl + IG chevron crawl |
| 3 | +50 | ~175 | UK/EU pass (city queries, #MusesofAM, EU hashtags) |
| 4 | +50 | ~225 | bridal + lab-grown verticals; YouTube/Substack |
| 5 | +25–50 | **250+** | Modash trial export to backfill weakest niches |

Weekly ops rhythm: Mon — 1h mining (add ~15); Tue — 1h qualification (move to_review → to_contact, verify counts + ER); Wed — 1h outreach DMs/emails (20–30 sends); Fri — 30min tracker hygiene (statuses, posted links, notes). Expected funnel: ~50% of to_contact respond → ~50% of responders agree → gift 25–40/month → 60–80% post rate with a good unboxing moment.

## 10. Tracker hygiene rules
- One row per handle; `source` must be a URL you actually saw the handle in.
- Never add a handle you haven't seen with your own eyes on-platform or in a source — no guessed handles.
- Mark any count not read directly off the platform `est_unverified`; re-verify at to_contact stage.
- Log every DM date and reply in `notes`; move status the same day something changes.
