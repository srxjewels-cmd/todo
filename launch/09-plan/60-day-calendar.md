# [BRAND] — 60-Day Launch Calendar

**Brand:** [BRAND] — 18k gold vermeil + IGI-certified lab-grown diamonds, made to order, fair-value narrative.
**Audience:** Women 25–40, US/UK/EU. **Target gross margin:** ~75% on lab pieces.
**Plan start:** Week of Mon 2026-07-06 (written Wed 2026-07-08 — Week 1 is a compressed 5-day sprint).

**Legend:** `[F]` = founder-only (cannot delegate) · `[D]` = delegatable (VA, freelancer, agency)

Companion files: `kpi-dashboard.md` (definitions, formulas, targets) and `kpi-tracker.csv` (weekly log — fill every Monday).

---

## Week 1 — Foundations (Mon 2026-07-06 → Sun 2026-07-12)

**Objectives:** Lock the brand identity, secure the digital footprint, get physical product in motion, and open the top of the funnel — all four must land this week or everything downstream slips.

**Tasks:**
- `[F]` Lock the brand name. Decision criteria: trademark search clear in US/UK/EU (USPTO TESS, UKIPO, EUIPO), .com available or acquirable ≤ $2k, Instagram + TikTok handles free. Decide by Thu 2026-07-09 — this blocks everything.
- `[F]` Buy domain + variants (.com, .co.uk if cheap); register @handle on Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube.
- `[F]` Place sample orders with manufacturer: full launch SKU set (see `../02-skus/`) in the hero finish, plus 2–3 size runs for rings. Pay rush fees if needed — samples must arrive within 7–10 days. Confirm IGI certificates ship with lab-diamond samples.
- `[F]` Approve final launch SKU list + pricing (fair-value narrative requires you to sign off on the "true cost vs. retail markup" math per piece).
- `[D]` Stand up Shopify store (password-protected): theme installed, payment + shipping zones (US/UK/EU), taxes/VAT configured, legal pages drafted.
- `[D]` Waitlist live by Fri 2026-07-10: landing page (Shopify password page or dedicated page) + Klaviyo signup form + welcome flow (email 1 of the pre-launch series). Offer: founding-customer perk (early access + founding price or gift-with-purchase).
- `[D]` Set up analytics day one: Shopify Analytics, Meta Pixel + Conversions API, TikTok Pixel, GA4, Klaviyo-Shopify integration. Verify test events fire.
- `[D]` Build creator seeding target list to 150 names (micro 5k–100k, women 25–40, jewellery/fashion/lifestyle, US/UK/EU) with contact info in a tracker sheet (see `../07-creators/`).
- `[F]` Record founder story video (raw, phone-shot): why lab-grown, why fair pricing. This is the seed of all launch content — only you can tell it.

**Exit criteria:**
- [ ] Name locked, domain live, handles secured
- [ ] Sample PO confirmed with arrival date ≤ 2026-07-17
- [ ] Waitlist page live and capturing emails into Klaviyo
- [ ] Pixels verified firing on the waitlist page
- [ ] Creator list ≥ 150 names

**Metric that matters:** Waitlist signups (target: first 100 by Sunday, from personal network + founder story posted on personal socials).

## Week 2 — Samples Arrive, Shoot, Seeding Wave 1 (Mon 2026-07-13 → Sun 2026-07-19)

**Objectives:** Turn physical samples into a content library and get the first 50 boxes into creators' hands.

**Tasks:**
- `[F]` QC every sample on arrival: vermeil thickness/finish, stone setting, IGI cert match, sizing accuracy. Reject/reorder anything below standard — this is the product bar only you can set.
- `[F]` Approve the "fair-value breakdown" card that goes in every box (cost transparency insert) — core brand claim, founder sign-off required.
- `[D]` Product shoot (2 days): white-background PDP shots (every SKU, every angle), lifestyle/on-model shots, macro detail of settings and hallmark, IGI certificate beauty shots. Hire photographer + hand model.
- `[D]` UGC-style phone content same week: try-on, unboxing, stacking, "what I ordered vs. what came."
- `[D]` Seeding wave 1 — ship 50 boxes: personalized note, fair-value card, no hard posting obligation but a clear ask ("tag us if you love it"), unique vanity code per creator (e.g., ANNA15) created in Shopify. Log every shipment in the creator tracker (name, ship date, code).
- `[D]` Load PDPs into Shopify: photography, copy (fair-value price breakdown module on every PDP), IGI cert numbers, made-to-order lead times stated clearly.
- `[D]` Waitlist growth: 3–4 organic posts/week from shoot outtakes; founder story video cut for TikTok/Reels.
- `[F]` Personally DM/email the top 20 creators on the list — founder outreach converts far better than templated agency mail.

**Exit criteria:**
- [ ] Samples QC'd and approved (or reorders in flight)
- [ ] ≥ 300 finished assets (photo + video) in the library
- [ ] 50 boxes shipped, all logged with vanity codes
- [ ] All PDPs live behind password

**Metric that matters:** Boxes shipped (target: 50 by Friday 2026-07-17).

## Week 3 — Content Engine On, Waitlist Growth, Seeding Wave 2 (Mon 2026-07-20 → Sun 2026-07-26)

**Objectives:** Move from ad-hoc posting to a repeatable content cadence, accelerate the waitlist, and ship the second seeding wave while wave-1 posts start landing.

**Tasks:**
- `[D]` Content engine: 1 TikTok/day + 4–5 Reels/week + 3 feed posts/week from the Week-2 library. Formats to rotate: fair-value cost breakdowns, IGI cert explainers ("what lab-grown actually is"), unboxings, founder POV, styling/stacking.
- `[D]` Seeding wave 2 — ship next 50–75 boxes (cumulative ~100–125). Prioritize lookalikes of wave-1 creators who posted.
- `[D]` Repost/whitelist every creator post within 24h; ask top posters for Spark Ads / partnership-ad permissions now (needed Week 5).
- `[D]` Waitlist accelerators: giveaway (win a founding piece — entry = email + follow), referral incentive in Klaviyo welcome flow ("skip the line"), waitlist counter/urgency on landing page.
- `[D]` Klaviyo pre-launch nurture live: 3-email series (founder story / how lab-grown + vermeil is made, with the cost math / what's coming at launch + founding-customer mechanics).
- `[F]` Review wave-1 creator content: which hooks/formats are getting saves and comments? Pick the 3 winning angles to double down on — creative judgment call.
- `[F]` Set soft-launch date and founding offer mechanics (sign-off): early-access window length, founding price or perk, quantity caps per SKU given made-to-order capacity.
- `[D]` Ops check: confirm manufacturer capacity + lead times for launch volume; finalize packaging inventory; set up customer-support inbox + FAQ (sizing, lead times, returns, vermeil care).

**Exit criteria:**
- [ ] 7-day content streak maintained on TikTok + IG
- [ ] Cumulative boxes shipped ≥ 100
- [ ] ≥ 10 creator posts live (20%+ of wave 1)
- [ ] Pre-launch email series live; waitlist growing ≥ 300/week

**Metric that matters:** Waitlist size (target: ≥ 1,000 total by Sunday 2026-07-26).

## Week 4 — Soft Launch to Waitlist (Mon 2026-07-27 → Sun 2026-08-02)

**Objectives:** Open the store to the waitlist only. Founding-customer mechanics convert the list, generate first revenue, first reviews, and stress-test made-to-order ops before paid traffic.

**Tasks:**
- `[F]` Go/no-go decision Mon 2026-07-27: site QA'd, inventory/capacity confirmed, support ready. You own the call.
- `[D]` Founding-customer mechanics live: unique early-access link or password to waitlist only; founding perk (e.g., founding price locked for life, engraved founding number, or GWP); 72-hour early-access window before anyone else.
- `[D]` Klaviyo launch sequence: T-48h "doors open soon," T-0 "you're in," +24h social proof, +48h "early access closes tonight." SMS on the T-0 send if numbers were collected.
- `[F]` Personally reply to/thank the first 50 customers (email or handwritten note in box). Founding cohort loyalty is built by the founder.
- `[D]` Ops drill: confirm every order into production same-day; send "your piece is being made" email with realistic ship date (made-to-order transparency is part of the brand).
- `[D]` Capture everything: screenshot sales notifications, film packing orders — this is Week 5 launch content.
- `[D]` Post-purchase review flow armed in Klaviyo (trigger: delivered + 3 days); photo reviews incentivized.
- `[D]` Build launch-day ad account structure (campaigns, audiences, creatives loaded, NOT live): Meta ASC+ prospecting + a retargeting stack; TikTok Smart+ equivalent. Get whitelisting/Spark permissions finalized.
- `[F]` Watch soft-launch data daily: which SKUs convert, AOV, size-curve issues, checkout drop-off. Decide any pricing/PDP fixes before public launch.

**Exit criteria:**
- [ ] Waitlist conversion ≥ 5% (see kpi-dashboard.md; 5–15% is the benchmark band)
- [ ] Zero unresolved fulfillment/checkout failures
- [ ] ≥ 10 reviews requested, first reviews inbound
- [ ] Ad account fully built and in review, ready to flip on

**Metric that matters:** Waitlist → customer conversion rate (target: ≥ 5%, stretch 10%).

## Week 5 — Public Launch + Ads On (Mon 2026-08-03 → Sun 2026-08-09)

**Objectives:** Open to the public, turn on paid, and make launch week loud — creators, email, organic, and ads all firing on the same 7 days.

**Tasks:**
- `[F]` Flip the store public Mon 2026-08-03; post the founder launch video (personal + brand accounts).
- `[D]` Ads live Day 1 at modest spend (e.g., $150–300/day total across Meta + TikTok): Meta ASC+ with 8–12 creatives (best organic/creator hooks), TikTok with Spark Ads on top creator posts. Do NOT scale until 50+ purchases of signal.
- `[D]` Coordinate creators: ask wave 1–2 posters to post launch-week content with their vanity codes; ship "launch week" top-up boxes to the top 10 performers.
- `[D]` Klaviyo public-launch series to non-buyers + full list; announce founding window closed → normal pricing (scarcity proof).
- `[D]` PR/affiliate light-touch: submit to product-launch newsletters, jewellery/fashion editors with lab-grown fair-value angle.
- `[F]` Daily 15-min ad check with media buyer (or solo): kill creatives with CTR < 0.8% or CPA > 2× target after ~1.5× AOV in spend; protect learning phase otherwise.
- `[D]` Retargeting live by Day 3 (site visitors, video viewers, ATC abandoners) with review/UGC creative.
- `[D]` Support SLA: all tickets < 12h during launch week.

**Exit criteria:**
- [ ] Ads spending consistently with pixel/CAPI match quality confirmed
- [ ] First-week blended ROAS ≥ 1.33 (break-even; see kpi-dashboard.md)
- [ ] ≥ 25 public-launch orders beyond founding cohort
- [ ] No stockout/capacity breach on hero SKUs

**Metric that matters:** Blended ROAS (target: ≥ 1.33 break-even, aim 1.5–2.0 in week 1).

## Week 6 — Creative Iteration I (Mon 2026-08-10 → Sun 2026-08-16)

**Objectives:** First real performance data is in — iterate creative, fix the funnel, keep the content engine running.

**Tasks:**
- `[D]` Creative sprint 1: produce 8–10 new ad variants iterating on winners only (new hooks on winning bodies, new bodies on winning hooks). Kill the bottom 50% of launch creatives.
- `[D]` Funnel fixes from data: PDP scroll/heatmap review, checkout drop-off, mobile speed, size-guide friction. Ship top 3 fixes.
- `[D]` Seeding wave 3: +50 boxes (cumulative ~150–175), targeted at niches where codes actually converted (check vanity-code data by niche).
- `[F]` Review week-1 cohort quality: which SKUs drive first orders, refund/remake rate, support themes. Decide any SKU price/mix adjustments.
- `[D]` UGC pipeline: brief 3–5 paid UGC creators (distinct from seeding) for whitelisted ad content — organic seeding alone won't feed the ad account forever.
- `[D]` Email: launch the post-purchase flow review pass (delivery-time expectations for made-to-order), start weekly campaign rhythm (2 sends/week: 1 story/education, 1 product).
- `[F]` 30-min Monday KPI review (see kpi-dashboard.md ritual) — from this week on, non-negotiable.

**Exit criteria:**
- [ ] ≥ 8 new ad creatives live; bottom performers paused
- [ ] Top 3 funnel fixes shipped
- [ ] Cumulative boxes ≥ 150; creator post rate holding ≥ 20%

**Metric that matters:** CVR (target: ≥ 1.0% sitewide; demi-fine benchmark band 1–2.5%).

## Week 7 — Creative Iteration II + Scale Seeding (Mon 2026-08-17 → Sun 2026-08-23)

**Objectives:** Scale what's working — spend up behind winning creative, push seeding toward the 250-box goal, deepen email revenue.

**Tasks:**
- `[D]` Scale spend +20–30% on campaigns holding CPA ≤ ceiling (see kpi-dashboard.md CAC section); hold or trim the rest. Test 1 new audience/geo expansion (e.g., UK if US-led, or vice versa).
- `[D]` Creative sprint 2: another 8–10 variants; start a distinct lab-diamond-hero angle vs. vermeil-entry angle to learn which drives higher-AOV orders.
- `[D]` Seeding wave 4: +50–75 boxes (cumulative ~225). Introduce a light affiliate tier for proven posters (10–15% commission on their vanity code) to convert seeding into an ongoing channel.
- `[D]` Klaviyo: browse-abandon and back-in-stock/waitlist-by-SKU flows live; A/B founding-style storytelling vs. product-led campaigns.
- `[F]` Negotiate with manufacturer using 6 weeks of real volume: unit costs, lead-time commitments, capacity reservation for Q4. Only the founder carries this.
- `[F]` Review contribution margin per channel (paid vs. creator codes vs. email vs. organic) and set Week 8+ budget split.
- `[D]` Start collecting founding-customer testimonials + photos for evergreen ads and PDPs.

**Exit criteria:**
- [ ] Cumulative boxes ≥ 225 and on track to 250
- [ ] Paid CAC at or under ceiling at increased spend
- [ ] Email revenue share ≥ 15% and climbing toward the 20–30% target

**Metric that matters:** CAC vs. ceiling (blended CAC ≤ ~60% of the CAC ceiling from kpi-dashboard.md).

## Week 8 — 250 Boxes + First Repeat-Purchase Data (Mon 2026-08-24 → Sun 2026-08-30)

**Objectives:** Hit 250 cumulative seeded boxes, read the first repeat-purchase signal from the founding cohort (~4 weeks post soft launch), and lock the Month-3 plan.

**Tasks:**
- `[D]` Seeding wave 5: final +25–50 boxes to reach ≥ 250 cumulative. Publish internal recap: post rate, revenue per box, top-10 creators → move them to the affiliate/ambassador tier.
- `[F]` Repeat-purchase analysis (founding cohort is now 4 weeks old): repeat rate, days-to-second-order, what they buy second (stack piece? upgrade to lab diamond?). This sets your true LTV and therefore your real CAC ceiling — founder decision.
- `[D]` Launch the second-purchase engine in Klaviyo based on that data: cross-sell flow (e.g., "complete the stack") triggered at the observed median repurchase window.
- `[D]` Creative sprint 3 + first scaling structure cleanup: consolidate winners, retire fatigued ads (frequency > 3), refresh retargeting.
- `[F]` 60-day retro + Month-3 plan: channel budget split, SKU roadmap (bestseller extensions, drop cadence), hiring needs (in-house content? CX?), cash position vs. made-to-order working capital.
- `[D]` Ops hardening: made-to-order lead-time actuals vs. promised; remake/return rate; update PDP promises to match reality.

**Exit criteria:**
- [ ] ≥ 250 cumulative boxes shipped; post rate ≥ 20% lifetime
- [ ] Repeat-purchase rate measured and second-purchase flow live
- [ ] Month-3 plan written with budget allocations

**Metric that matters:** Repeat purchase rate of founding cohort (any repeat ≥ 5–8% at 4 weeks is a strong early signal for jewellery).
