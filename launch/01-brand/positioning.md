## 3. Fair-value narrative doctrine

### The "honest math" story

This is the brand's core argument. Tell it, in some form, everywhere.

1. **Lab-grown costs less to make — so it should cost you less.** A lab-grown diamond is chemically and optically a diamond, graded by IGI like any other. It costs a fraction of a mined stone to produce. We pass that difference on instead of pocketing it behind the word "luxury."
2. **Made to order means no clearance rack.** Sales exist to liquidate inventory. We hold none — every piece is started when you order it and ships in about seven days. No stock to clear means no reason to ever mark down.
3. **Direct means one margin, not three.** No wholesaler, no department-store cut, no keystone-on-keystone markup. You pay materials + certification + craft + one fair margin.
4. **Therefore: the price is the price.** It was set honestly on day one, so it never needs to go on sale. That's not stubbornness — it's the receipt.

One-line version: **"Lab-grown + made-to-order + direct = the price. That's the whole trick. There is no trick."**

### 10 messaging DOs

1. DO show the math: name what the price pays for (18k gold, sterling silver, IGI-certified lab-grown diamond, a maker's seven days).
2. DO say "real" and prove it in the same breath — thickness in microns, certification by report number, hallmark on the silver.
3. DO always write "lab-grown diamond" — the words "lab-grown" immediately before "diamond," every single time, in every channel.
4. DO treat the ~7-day wait as a feature: "made for you" is the reason there's no warehouse and no markdowns.
5. DO compare us to *our own promise* ("what we said, what we did"), publishing specs and reports rather than adjectives.
6. DO acknowledge trade-offs plainly (vermeil vs. solid gold, lab-grown vs. mined) and let the customer choose informed.
7. DO celebrate self-purchase as the default story — she is the buyer, the occasion and the recipient.
8. DO speak about natural diamonds with respect; our one natural-diamond halo piece is a special collection, not a repentance.
9. DO answer objections in public — FAQ, product pages, DMs — with the exact counter-messages above.
10. DO frame the constant price as the benefit: "the price you see in July is the price in December — buying early never punishes you."

### 10 messaging DON'Ts

1. DON'T ever show "% off," strikethrough prices, "was/now," or "compare at" — anywhere, ever.
2. DON'T manufacture urgency: no countdown timers, no "only 3 left" (made-to-order means nothing is ever running out), no "sale ends tonight."
3. DON'T use the word "cheap," or its cousins "bargain," "steal," "dupe" — we are fairly priced, not cheap.
4. DON'T disparage natural diamonds — no "mined diamonds are a scam / blood diamonds / rip-off" framing; we win with honesty, not by trashing the alternative.
5. DON'T call vermeil "gold" without the word "vermeil," and never bury the sterling-silver core — over-claiming is the fastest way to lose this customer.
6. DON'T write "diamond" alone for lab stones — no "our diamonds," no "diamond studs" unqualified; it is always "lab-grown diamond."
7. DON'T promise investment or resale value on any piece.
8. DON'T use luxury-theatre clichés: "exclusive," "elevated," "indulge," "treat yourself like a queen."
9. DON'T dress the founding-member perk up as a discount, credit, or "founder pricing" — it is access and a gift, full stop.
10. DON'T overstate speed or certification — it's "ships in about 7 days," and IGI reports are on finished jewellery; say exactly that.

### Price-integrity rules

1. **No launch discount. No first-order discount. No newsletter-signup discount.** The launch price is the honest price; discounting it on day one would falsify the entire brand.
2. **Founding-member perk = access + gift, never money off.** Examples: first access to new drops, a reserved production slot, complimentary engraving, a care kit or travel case, name credited in the founding list. Nothing that implies the price was ever padded.
3. **No sales calendar.** No Black Friday percentage, no January sale, no birthday codes. If we participate in retail moments at all, it's with a gift-with-purchase or a new piece, never a markdown.
4. **Prices change only when costs change** — gold, silver, stones, labour — and we say so publicly when they do, in either direction.
5. **No strikethrough anything.** Not on site, not in ads, not in influencer briefs. Affiliate and creator partners get gifting and tracked links, never discount codes.
6. **One price worldwide logic:** currency-adjusted, tax-transparent, no region-based games.
7. **If we ever misprice, we honour or refund transparently** — we do not quietly edit.

## 4. Voice & tone

### Three adjectives

1. **Candid** — we volunteer the details other brands bury (microns, cores, costs, trade-offs).
2. **Assured** — calm and unhurried; we never beg, rush, or shout, because nothing here is about to sell out.
3. **Warm** — precise doesn't mean cold; we sound like a knowledgeable friend, not a spec sheet or a sales floor.

### Before / after copy examples

**1. Product description**
- *Generic voice:* "This stunning must-have tennis bracelet will elevate any look. Crafted with dazzling stones and a luxurious gold finish, it's the ultimate treat-yourself piece!"
- *[BRAND] voice:* "A line of IGI-certified lab-grown diamonds set in 18k gold vermeil — 2.5+ microns of real gold over solid sterling silver. Made to your order in about seven days. That's the whole story; it doesn't need adjectives."

**2. Email subject + opener**
- *Generic voice:* "LAST CHANCE: 40% off everything ends at midnight! Don't miss out on the sale of the season!"
- *[BRAND] voice:* "Our price today is our price in December. New pieces just opened for ordering — here's exactly what each one is made of, and why it costs what it costs."

**3. Low-stock / urgency module**
- *Generic voice:* "Hurry — only 3 left in stock! Order now before it's gone forever!"
- *[BRAND] voice:* "Nothing here is running out. Every piece is made to order, so take your time — yours begins the day you do."
