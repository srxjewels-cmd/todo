## Shipping Policy

**Every [BRAND] piece is made to order — for you, from scratch.**

We don't hold mass-produced stock. When you place your order, our workshop begins crafting your piece: casting, setting your IGI-certified lab-grown diamonds, finishing in 18k gold vermeil over sterling silver, 2.5 microns+, and completing quality control.

### Production time

- **Crafting: approximately 7 business days** from order confirmation.
- You'll receive an email when your piece enters production and another when it ships.
- If your order is a gift with a deadline, contact us at hello@[BRAND].com and we'll do our best to prioritise it.

### Delivery — insured express shipping

Once crafted, every order ships via **fully insured, tracked express courier**. A signature is required on delivery for your protection.

| Region | Delivery after dispatch | Total estimate from order |
|---|---|---|
| United States | 2–3 business days | ~9–10 business days |
| United Kingdom | 1–2 business days | ~8–9 business days |
| European Union | 2–4 business days | ~9–11 business days |

Estimates are typical, not guaranteed. Peak periods (holidays) may add 1–3 business days to production.

### Duties & taxes (UK and EU orders) — no surprise fees

We ship **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** to the UK and EU wherever available: all applicable import duties and VAT are calculated and collected at checkout, so **the price you see at checkout is the final price**. Your courier will never ask you to pay customs charges on the doorstep. If a customs charge is ever incorrectly levied on a DDP shipment, contact us with the receipt and we will reimburse you in full.

US orders: applicable state sales tax is calculated at checkout.

### Tracking, insurance & delivery issues

- Every shipment is insured door-to-door at full value.
- Tracking details are emailed at dispatch and available in your account.
- If your order arrives damaged, or does not arrive within 5 business days of the courier's estimate, email hello@[BRAND].com within 48 hours and we will replace or refund it — the insurance claim is our job, not yours.

## Returns & Exchanges Policy

We want you to be certain. You have **30 days from the day of delivery** to return or exchange your piece.

### How to return

1. Start your return at **[BRAND].com/returns** (our returns portal) with your order number and email.
2. The portal issues a prepaid, tracked, insured return label:
   - **US customers** return to our US returns point (domestic label — no customs forms).
   - **UK & EU customers** return to our EU returns hub via our returns consolidator (local-rate label — no customs forms, no re-import paperwork for you).
3. Pack the piece in its original packaging **including the IGI report** and drop it off. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the piece passing inspection at our returns point.

### Return conditions

- Items must be unworn, in original condition, with all original packaging and the IGI report included.
- Return shipping via our portal label is free on your **first exchange**; for refunds, the cost of the return label ([X] US / [X] UK/EU) is deducted from your refund. Exchanged items ship back to you free.
- We do not refund original express shipping costs unless the item was faulty.

### Free first exchange

Wrong size, wrong length, changed your mind on the stone shape? Your **first exchange on any order is completely free** — free return label, free re-delivery of the new piece. Because every exchange is made to order, allow ~7 business days of production for the replacement.

### Resizing

- Rings: **one complimentary resize within 60 days of delivery** (portal → "Resize"). We cover the return label and re-delivery.
- After 60 days, or for additional resizes, we resize at cost (quoted through the portal).
- Some designs (full pavé bands, engraved bands) can only be resized within a limited range — this is noted on the product page.

### Exclusions

The following are **final sale** and cannot be returned or exchanged (except where faulty):

- Engraved or otherwise personalised pieces.
- Bespoke / custom commissions made to your specification.
- Gift cards.

**Your statutory rights are not affected.** UK and EU customers retain their statutory 14-day right of withdrawal on eligible items; our 30-day policy is more generous and applies in addition. Note that under UK/EU consumer law, goods personalised to your specification (engraving, bespoke commissions) are exempt from the statutory withdrawal right, consistent with the exclusions above. Faulty items are always covered regardless of exclusions.

### The natural-diamond halo piece

Our one natural-diamond halo special piece **is fully returnable within the same 30-day window**, provided it is returned with its IGI natural-diamond report. The report is part of the piece's value and identity — a return without the certificate cannot be accepted, so please keep it safe. (Replacement certificates can sometimes be arranged through IGI at your cost, but this delays any refund.)

## 2-Year Craftsmanship Warranty

Every [BRAND] piece is covered by a **2-year warranty against manufacturing defects**, from the date of delivery.

### What's covered

- Premature wear of the vermeil layer under normal wear (our 18k gold vermeil over sterling silver, 2.5 microns+ is built to last well beyond industry-standard plating).
- Stones loosening or falling out of their settings under normal wear.
- Clasp, jump-ring, post or structural failures under normal wear.

If a covered defect appears within 2 years, we will **repair or replace the piece free of charge**, including insured shipping both ways.

### What's not covered

- Loss or theft (we recommend insuring your piece — the IGI report makes this easy).
- Damage from misuse: chemical exposure (perfume, chlorine, cleaning products), abrasion, impact, or improper storage.
- Normal surface patina and the gradual, gentle wear that any gold surface develops with daily life.
- Repairs or alterations carried out by third parties (these void the warranty).

To make a claim: email hello@[BRAND].com with your order number and photos. We'll take it from there.

## Your IGI Certificate

**Every [BRAND] piece ships with its own IGI (International Gemological Institute) finished-jewellery report**, independently documenting the lab-grown diamonds set in your piece (and the natural diamonds in our halo special piece). Keep it with your purchase record — you'll need it for insurance, resizing, warranty service and any return. There is nothing to register and nothing to activate; the certificate is yours from day one.
