# Lab-Grown Diamonds 101

Everything you actually want to know — including the parts other brands skip.

## What is a lab-grown diamond?

A lab-grown diamond is a diamond — carbon atoms in the same crystal structure, grown above ground instead of mined from it. Chemically, optically and physically, lab-grown diamonds are identical to natural diamonds: the same hardness (10 on the Mohs scale), the same brilliance, the same fire. A jeweller's loupe can't tell them apart; it takes specialised laboratory equipment to distinguish a lab-grown diamond from a natural diamond.

There are two ways to grow one:

- **HPHT (High Pressure, High Temperature)** recreates the conditions deep in the Earth where natural diamonds formed — a small diamond seed, carbon, and presses exerting enormous pressure at around 1,500°C.
- **CVD (Chemical Vapour Deposition)** grows a lab-grown diamond layer by layer inside a vacuum chamber, as carbon atoms from an energised gas settle onto a diamond seed plate.

Both methods produce the real thing: a lab-grown diamond, not an imitation. (Cubic zirconia and moissanite, by contrast, are different materials entirely — simulants that only look similar.)

## How IGI grades our lab-grown diamonds

Every [BRAND] piece is assessed by the **International Gemological Institute (IGI)**, one of the world's largest independent gem laboratories and the leading authority on grading lab-grown diamonds.

IGI grades lab-grown diamonds on the same **4Cs** used for natural diamonds:

- **Carat** — weight (and therefore size).
- **Colour** — how close to colourless, from D (colourless) downward.
- **Clarity** — internal characteristics visible under 10x magnification.
- **Cut** — the precision of proportions and finish, which determines sparkle.

Because our lab-grown diamonds are set into finished pieces, your certificate is an **IGI finished-jewellery report**: IGI examines the completed piece and documents the mounted stones' characteristics, the metal, and the piece itself. ## Lab-grown vs natural — the honest version

Plenty of brands oversell one side or the other. Here's the straight answer.

**Where they're the same.** Composition, hardness, brilliance, durability. A lab-grown diamond will not fade, cloud, or "wear out" — it is a diamond, permanently.

**Where they differ.**

- **Origin and rarity.** Natural diamonds formed over roughly a billion years underground and are finite. Lab-grown diamonds are grown in weeks and supply keeps expanding. If billion-year rarity is part of the meaning for you, that's a legitimate reason to choose natural — which is why we offer one natural-diamond halo special piece.
- **Price.** Lab-grown diamonds cost a fraction of comparable natural diamonds — that's what lets us set genuinely well-cut, well-graded stones at demi-fine prices.
- **Resale value — read this part.** Lab-grown diamond prices have fallen substantially as production has scaled, and they may keep falling. A lab-grown diamond is not an investment, and you should not expect meaningful resale value. (Honesty compels us to add: natural diamonds bought at retail also typically resell well below purchase price — jewellery in general is a poor investment vehicle.) Buy a lab-grown diamond for what it is: a beautiful, certified, real stone you'll actually wear — at a price that respects the fact you're buying jewellery, not an asset.

We'd rather tell you this ourselves than have you read it elsewhere.

## What is 18k gold vermeil?

Our pieces are crafted in **18k gold vermeil over sterling silver, 2.5 microns+**.

Vermeil (pronounced "ver-may") is a legally defined standard, not a marketing word: a **sterling silver** base coated with real gold of at least 10 karats at a substantial minimum thickness of 2.5 microns. It is precious metal through and through — silver core, thick 18k gold surface.

How it compares:

- **Gold-plated** fashion jewellery: typically brass or steel base with a gold flash often under 0.5 microns — it can wear through quickly.
- **[BRAND] vermeil**: sterling silver base, 18k gold at 2.5 microns and above — several times thicker, built for years of wear, and backed by our 2-year craftsmanship warranty.
- **Solid gold**: gold throughout. Beautiful, and 3–10x the price for the same design. Vermeil is how we keep fine-jewellery quality at demi-fine prices.

## Caring for your piece

Your lab-grown diamonds are nearly indestructible; the vermeil deserves a little kindness.

- **Last on, first off.** Put jewellery on after perfume, lotion and hairspray; take it off before bed.
- **Keep it dry-ish.** Remove before swimming (chlorine and salt water), showering, workouts and cleaning with household chemicals.
- **Clean gently.** Wipe with the soft cloth included in your box. For your lab-grown diamonds, a soft brush with lukewarm water and a drop of mild soap, then pat dry. No ultrasonic cleaners, no abrasive polishing cloths made for solid metals.
- **Store smart.** Each piece in its own pouch or compartment — even your own lab-grown diamonds can scratch the gold surface of a neighbouring piece.

Cared for this way, vermeil keeps its colour for years — and it's covered by our 2-year craftsmanship warranty either way.

## FAQ

**Are lab-grown diamonds real diamonds?**
Yes. Lab-grown diamonds have the same chemical composition, crystal structure, hardness and optical properties as natural diamonds. The only difference is origin — grown above ground rather than mined. They are always identified as lab-grown diamonds, which is exactly how we label them, everywhere.

**Will a lab-grown diamond pass a diamond tester?**
Yes. Standard thermal/electrical testers read lab-grown diamonds as diamond, because they are diamond. Simulants like cubic zirconia fail; lab-grown diamonds don't.

**Can anyone tell my stone is lab-grown?**
Not by looking, and not with a loupe. Only advanced laboratory instruments can distinguish lab-grown diamonds from natural diamonds. Many certified stones also carry a microscopic girdle inscription identifying them — invisible to the naked eye.

**Will my lab-grown diamond fade, cloud, or change over time?**
No. A lab-grown diamond is permanent. It won't fade, yellow or lose its sparkle — a quick clean restores day-one brilliance after everyday film from lotions builds up.

**What exactly is an IGI finished-jewellery report?**
An independent laboratory document from IGI assessing your completed piece: the mounted lab-grown diamonds' carat weight, colour, clarity and cut, plus the metal and setting. One ships with every [BRAND] order. It's your proof of quality for insurance and any future resale or service.

**Is vermeil the same as gold-plated?**
No. Vermeil is a regulated standard requiring a sterling silver base and a substantial gold layer of at least 2.5 microns. Typical gold plating uses base metals like brass with a far thinner gold layer. Ours is 18k gold vermeil over sterling silver, 2.5 microns+.

**Will lab-grown diamonds hold their value?**
Honestly: don't count on it. Lab-grown diamond prices have declined as supply has grown. Buy for beauty, certification and fair pricing today — not as an investment. (Retail natural diamonds are also generally poor investments; we just say the quiet part out loud.)

**Are lab-grown diamonds eco-friendly?**
We don't make that claim. Growing lab-grown diamonds takes significant energy, and the footprint depends on how each grower sources power. Unless we can prove an environmental claim, we won't make it — our promise is fair value and honest labelling, full stop.

Our single halo special piece features natural diamonds, ships with its IGI natural-diamond report, and is priced with the same fair-value discipline — never discounted.
