# [BRAND] — Advertising & Product-Claim Compliance Guide

**Audience:** everyone who writes anything customer-facing — PDPs, ads, emails, social, packaging, support macros.
**Golden rule:** the qualifier **"lab-grown" must IMMEDIATELY precede the word "diamond"** in every reference to our lab stones. Everywhere. No exceptions. Titles, body copy, alt text, meta descriptions, ads, hashtags, filters, support replies.

---

## 1. FTC Jewelry Guides — lab-grown diamond claims (US)

The FTC Guides for the Jewelry, Precious Metals, and Pewter Industries (16 CFR Part 23) govern how we may describe diamonds. The 2018 revision recognises lab-grown diamonds as diamonds — **but only when clearly and conspicuously qualified.**

### 1.1 The qualifier rule

- The word "diamond" used alone implies a natural, mined stone. For our lab stones, a clear qualifier must **immediately precede** the word "diamond".
- [BRAND] house qualifier: **"lab-grown"** (FTC also accepts "laboratory-grown", "laboratory-created", "[manufacturer name]-created"; we standardise on "lab-grown" for consistency).
- The qualifier must be **equally conspicuous** — same sentence, immediately adjacent, not buried in a footnote, tooltip, or linked page. An asterisk is not a qualifier.

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| "1 ct diamond solitaire" | "1 ct lab-grown diamond solitaire" |
| "Our diamonds are IGI-certified" (about lab stones) | "Our lab-grown diamonds are IGI-certified" |
| "Diamond studs" (title) + "lab-grown" in description | "Lab-grown diamond studs" (title) |
| "grown diamonds" / "created diamonds" | "lab-grown diamonds" |

### 1.2 Banned descriptors for lab stones

**Never** describe lab-grown diamonds as:

- **"cultured diamonds"** — the FTC treats "cultured" alone as deceptive for lab stones; do not use it at all, even qualified. It's not in our vocabulary.
- **"real diamonds" / "genuine diamonds" / "natural diamonds"** — never use "real", "genuine", "authentic", or "natural" adjacent to "diamond" for lab stones **without the lab-grown qualifier immediately preceding "diamond"**. "Real lab-grown diamonds" is acceptable (the qualifier still immediately precedes "diamond"); "real diamonds" for lab stones is not. **"Natural" is never used for lab stones in any construction** — "natural diamond" is reserved exclusively for the natural-diamond halo special piece.
- **"mined-equivalent" price comparisons** implying the customer is buying a natural stone.

Conversely, our lab-grown diamonds are **not** simulants — do not let copy drift toward "diamond-like", "diamond simulant", or comparisons that lump them with cubic zirconia or moissanite.

### 1.3 Eco / sustainability claims — FTC Green Guides

- **No unqualified environmental claims.** Do not say "eco-friendly", "sustainable", "ethical", "conflict-free", "green", or "carbon-neutral" about lab-grown diamonds without competent and reliable substantiation covering the specific claim. The FTC has sent warning letters to lab-grown diamond sellers for exactly this.
- Growing lab-grown diamonds is energy-intensive; environmental footprint depends on the grower's energy source. Unless we hold verified data from our specific supplier (e.g., third-party certified renewable energy), **we make no environmental claims at all.**
- Approved narrative instead: **fair value** — "fine-jewellery quality at demi-fine prices", made to order, no mined-stone markup. Value claims, not virtue claims.

## 2. Vermeil claims

"Vermeil" is a regulated term under the FTC Guides (16 CFR 23.5). To call a product vermeil:

- The base must be **sterling silver**;
- Coated on all significant surfaces with **gold of at least 10k fineness**;
- Of **substantial thickness** — minimum 2.5 microns (100 millionths of an inch).

[BRAND] spec meets and exceeds this. **Exact required wording, verbatim, on every PDP and in every materials reference:**

> **18k gold vermeil over sterling silver, 2.5 microns+**

Rules:

- Always disclose **both the base metal and the thickness** together. "18k gold vermeil" alone in body copy is acceptable only where the full line also appears on the same page (it must appear in the PDP materials block).
- Never say "solid gold", "18k gold" unqualified, or "gold" alone for vermeil pieces. Never "gold-dipped" or "gold-plated" either — vermeil is our claim and it's a stronger, regulated one.
- If any future product uses a non-silver base or thinner plating, it is **not vermeil** and must not be called vermeil.

## 3. UK — CMA / ASA rules (also good hygiene for EU)

UK sales put us under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (enforced by the CMA, with fining powers) and the CAP Code (enforced by the ASA).

### 3.1 Pressure-selling and urgency

- **No fake countdown timers.** A timer may only appear if the stated deadline is real and the offer genuinely ends/changes at zero. Recurring or resetting timers are a named banned practice.
- **No false scarcity**: no "only 3 left!" unless it is literally true from live data — and note that as a made-to-order brand, stock scarcity claims are inherently misleading for us. Don't use them. "Made to order" is our honest urgency: order today, crafted in ~7 days.
- No fake popularity signals ("12 people are viewing this") unless real-time and true.

### 3.2 "Was/now" and reference pricing

- A "was" price must be genuine: actually charged, at that price, for a meaningful recent period, with meaningful sales volume, and the "now" period must not dwarf the "was" period.
- **[BRAND] policy makes this simple: we are fair-value and never discount. We do not use "was/now", strikethrough prices, RRP comparisons, sitewide sales, or intro pricing. Ever.** Any copy implying a discount is off-brand and non-compliant by policy.
- Permitted value framing: comparison to *category norms* only if substantiated and specific (e.g., "comparable solid-gold, natural-diamond pieces typically retail for 3–5x" needs a documented basis before use — default is to avoid).

### 3.3 Claims substantiation (CAP Code)

- Every objective claim ("insured shipping", "IGI-certified", "2.5 microns+") must be documentable before publication.
- "Lab-grown diamond" qualification rules above apply equally in the UK — the ASA treats unqualified "diamond" for lab stones as misleading.
- UK hallmarking: articles sold in the UK as silver over 7.78g must bear a UK-recognised hallmark. Confirm hallmarking status with the workshop before making any "sterling silver" weight-bearing claims on heavier pieces. (Founder action item.)

## 4. Standard IGI callout block

Use this block verbatim wherever certification is referenced (PDP, education page, packaging insert):

> **Independently certified by IGI.** Every [BRAND] piece ships with an IGI (International Gemological Institute) finished-jewellery report documenting the lab-grown diamonds set in your piece — carat weight, colour, clarity and cut — assessed by an independent laboratory, not by us. Our natural-diamond halo piece ships with its IGI natural-diamond report. Keep your certificate: it supports insurance, resale documentation, warranty service and returns.

## 5. PDP template — where each disclosure sits

Every product page follows this structure. Placement is part of compliance: qualifiers and material disclosures must be visible without clicking, scrolling into accordions, or hovering.

```
─────────────────────────────────────────────
PRODUCT TITLE  ← qualifier in the title itself
  "Aria Lab-Grown Diamond Solitaire Ring"

PRICE  ← plain price. No strikethroughs, no "was/now", no timers.

SHORT DESCRIPTION (above the fold, first sentence carries the qualifier)
  "A 0.5 ct IGI-certified lab-grown diamond, set in a knife-edge band."

MATERIALS BLOCK (visible, not in an accordion)
  Metal: 18k gold vermeil over sterling silver, 2.5 microns+   ← verbatim line
  Stone: lab-grown diamond, 0.50 ct, F colour, VS1 clarity, Excellent cut
  Certification: IGI finished-jewellery report (number ships with piece)

CERTIFICATION BLOCK
  [Standard IGI callout block — section 4, verbatim]

MADE-TO-ORDER + SHIPPING LINE
  "Made to order for you — crafted in ~7 business days, then insured
   express delivery. UK/EU: duties & taxes included at checkout."

LONGER DESCRIPTION / STORY  ← every stone mention says "lab-grown diamond";
                              no eco claims; fair-value narrative only.

ACCORDIONS (care, sizing, returns)  ← may repeat disclosures; may not be
                                      their only location.
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Hidden-but-required fields (same rules apply):
• SEO title & meta description → "lab-grown diamond", never bare "diamond"
• Image alt text → "lab-grown diamond solitaire ring", never bare "diamond"
• Structured data (Product schema "material"/"description") → qualified
• Collection names, tags, filters → "Lab-Grown Diamond Rings", never "Diamond Rings"
─────────────────────────────────────────────
EXCEPTION — the natural-diamond halo piece: uses "natural diamond" (never
"lab-grown") for its halo stones, and its PDP states explicitly that this
piece features natural diamonds, certified by an IGI natural-diamond report.
If it mixes natural and lab-grown stones, each stone type is identified
separately and unambiguously.
```

## 6. Pre-publish checklist

Run before any PDP, page, email, or ad goes live. All boxes must be ticked.

- [ ] Every instance of "diamond" referring to a lab stone has **"lab-grown" immediately preceding it** — including title, meta title, meta description, alt text, tags, URL handle, schema, and any ad copy.
- [ ] Site-wide text search for bare `diamond` performed; each hit is either correctly qualified or a legitimate "natural diamond" reference on the halo piece.
- [ ] Zero occurrences of: `cultured`, `real diamond`, `genuine diamond`, `natural diamond` (outside the halo piece), `eco`, `sustainab`, `ethical`, `conflict-free`, `carbon`.
- [ ] Materials line appears verbatim: **"18k gold vermeil over sterling silver, 2.5 microns+"** — base metal and thickness both present.
- [ ] No "solid gold", no unqualified "18k gold", no "gold-plated"/"gold-dipped".
- [ ] IGI callout block present and unedited; certificate type correct (finished-jewellery report; natural-diamond report for the halo piece).
- [ ] No strikethrough prices, "was/now", RRP comparisons, discount codes, or sale language.
- [ ] No countdown timers, stock counters, or popularity claims.
- [ ] Made-to-order timeline stated (~7 business days) and consistent with the Shipping Policy.
- [ ] UK/EU pages state duties & taxes included (DDP) consistently with checkout behaviour.
- [ ] Every objective claim on the page can be substantiated with a document we hold today.
- [ ] Second person has reviewed (four-eyes rule) and initialled the launch log.
